# 🎬 Murder at Marrowby Manor: Filming Scripts

Four short scenes to film at home and play on the TV during the game:

| Scene | When it plays | Length | Spoilers? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. The Birthday Dinner** (opening) | Start of the game | 4–5 min | None. Safe for everyone. |
| **2. The Truth, Ending 1** | Reveal, if the game picks ending 1 | 2–3 min | ⚠️ YES |
| **3. The Truth, Ending 2** | Reveal, if the game picks ending 2 | 2–3 min | ⚠️ YES |
| **4. The Truth, Ending 3** | Reveal, if the game picks ending 3 | 2–3 min | ⚠️ YES |

> ⚠️ **Spoiler warning.** Scenes 2–4 reveal who did it in each ending. The game picks one of the
> three endings at random, so knowing all three doesn't tell you the answer, but it does give you a big
> head start. Best options:
> - Film Scene 1 with the whole family, and have **one or two grown-ups who won't play** film the reveals, **or**
> - Film the reveals **after** you've played each ending once, **or**
> - Skip the reveal videos. The TV board shows the reveal as text and can read it aloud.

**Stop reading here** if you're a player who hasn't solved the case yet. Scene 1 is safe; the reveals are at the bottom.

---

## How to add your videos to the game

1. Upload each scene to YouTube as **Unlisted** (only people with the link can see it).
2. On the TV board (the iPad), in the lobby, open **🎬 Your filmed scenes** and paste each link.
   Ending 1 = `reveal_A`, Ending 2 = `reveal_B`, Ending 3 = `reveal_C`.
3. Tap **Save links**. The board plays them at the right moments. Links are saved on that iPad only.

## Cast

One person can play several parts (change hats!). The most important parts are **Ozzie**, **Pembrook**,
and a **Narrator** (voice only is fine).

| Character | Who they are | Costume / prop idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 👴 **Grandpa Ozzie Marrowby** | Turns 80 tonight. World-famous puzzle-maker. Playful, a bit mischievous. | Cardigan, bow tie, **gold pocket watch on a chain** |
| 🤵 **Pembrook** | The butler. Very proper. Notices everything. | Towel over one arm, white gloves |
| 👩‍💼 **Victoria Marrowby** | Ozzie's daughter. Runs Marrowby Games. Always on her phone. | Blazer, phone in hand |
| 🎩 **Felix Marrowby** | Ozzie's son. A stage magician whose shows keep getting cancelled. | Top hat, deck of cards |
| 🌻 **Aunt Ginny Marrowby** | Ozzie's little sister. Grows orchids. Sweet… mostly. | Gardening gloves, flower pot |
| 👩‍🍳 **Chef Bianca Bell** | The family cook for 20 years. Famous cocoa. | Apron, wooden spoon, **blue mug** labeled “OZ” |
| ✏️ **Lola Quill** | Ozzie's young assistant and puzzle partner. | Pencil behind ear, notebook |
| 🎲 **Rex Hartley** | Ozzie's rival game designer for 40 years. Showy. | Fancy coat with gold cufflinks, a big die |
| 🎙️ **Narrator** | Voice of the story. | — |

**Props:** birthday cake with candles · blue “OZ” mug · gold pocket watch · wooden box · a heavy trophy (any
statue; a chess knight is perfect) · old book · fake will (rolled paper) · candle · string · a clock sound effect
(search “clock crash sound effect”).

**Filming tips:** film sideways (landscape). Shoot in a quiet room with the lights on. Turn off the
TV and music. It's fine to read lines off a card held behind the camera. Overacting is encouraged!

## Scene 1: The Birthday Dinner (opening, no spoilers)

> These times and details match the clues in the game exactly, so please keep them the same.

**[A dining table. Candles. A big “Happy 80th” sign if you have one. Everyone except the Narrator is seated.
Ozzie is at the head of the table.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** Marrowby Manor. Saturday night. A snowstorm has buried every road for miles. Inside,
the Marrowby family has gathered for Grandpa Ozzie's 80th birthday. Everyone is smiling. Not everyone means it.

**[Quick shot of each guest as the Narrator introduces them. Each one does a little "tell" for the camera.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** His daughter Victoria, who runs the family game company.
**[VICTORIA glances up from her phone, fake-smiles, goes back to her phone.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** His son Felix, the magician.
**[FELIX does a card trick. It fails. A card falls on the floor. He kicks it under the table.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** His little sister, Aunt Ginny.
**[GINNY pats OZZIE's hand sweetly… then stares at him a little too long.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** Chef Bianca, who has made his cocoa every night for twenty years.
**[BIANCA sets down a plate and checks her phone, frowning. “No signal… again.”]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** Lola Quill, his brilliant assistant.
**[LOLA scribbles in a notebook, then snaps it shut when someone looks over.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** And Rex Hartley, his rival for forty years.
**[REX raises his glass with a big grin. His gold cufflinks sparkle.]**

**[OZZIE taps his glass with a spoon. Everyone goes quiet.]**

**OZZIE:** Thank you all for coming through the snow. Eighty years old! You know, I've made a thousand puzzles in
my life. But tonight… tonight I've made my best one yet.

**OZZIE:** At midnight, I am changing my will.
**[Gasps. VICTORIA puts her phone down.]**

**OZZIE:** And one of you… is in for a very big surprise.
**[He smiles mysteriously. Everyone looks at each other.]**

**REX:** Ozzie, you old fox. Always a showman.

**OZZIE:** *(winks)* Nothing is ever an accident in a Marrowby puzzle, Rex. Remember that.

**[CUT TO: a little later. PEMBROOK sets down the blue "OZ" mug in front of OZZIE.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** At a quarter to nine, Chef Bianca's famous cocoa arrived, in Ozzie's blue “OZ” mug,
like every night.

**[OZZIE sips happily, then picks up the empty mug and carries it off toward the kitchen.]**

**OZZIE:** *(calling)* Delicious as always, Bianca!

**[CUT TO: OZZIE back at the table. He checks his gold pocket watch, then writes something on his napkin.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** At ten past nine, Ozzie checked his pocket watch, scribbled a note on his napkin…

**OZZIE:** *(standing up)* Excuse me. I need to have a word with someone about the midnight surprise.

**[He leaves. The camera lingers on his empty chair.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** …and walked out of the Ballroom. The guests drifted off, one by one.

**[CUT TO: Same table, later. PEMBROOK carries in a birthday cake with candles. Only VICTORIA, LOLA, and
GINNY are at the table.]**

**PEMBROOK:** Cake is served! Has anyone seen the master?

**[Everyone shrugs. Suddenly: a huge CRASH sound effect. Everyone jumps.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** At exactly 9:40, a terrible crash rang out from the Clock Tower.

**GINNY:** What on earth was THAT?

**[REX hurries in, out of breath.]**

**REX:** Did I miss the cake?

**[CUT TO: A closed door (the "tower door"). PEMBROOK knocks.]**

**PEMBROOK:** Master Ozzie? *(rattles handle)* It's bolted from the inside! *(pushes hard with his shoulder)* At my age!

**[The door bursts open. PEMBROOK looks in, and his face falls. He takes off his glove slowly.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** At ten o'clock, Pembrook broke down the bolted tower door… and found Grandpa Ozzie beside
the fallen clock weight. He was gone.

**PEMBROOK:** *(quietly, to camera)* It looks like an accident. But the master always said… nothing is ever an
accident in a Marrowby puzzle.

**[Black screen.]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** The police can't reach Marrowby Manor until the snowplow comes at midnight. But Grandpa
Ozzie's grandchildren have just arrived through the snow. Six suspects. One culprit. Ninety minutes.
Detectives… the investigation begins now.

**[END OF SCENE 1]**

## Scene 2: The Truth, Ending 1 (LOLA)

> *Lola, in the Study, with the Trick Puzzle Box, to keep a secret hidden.*

**[The Ballroom. All six suspects stand in a line. PEMBROOK stands in front of them like a detective.]**

**PEMBROOK:** Midnight. The snowplow is here, and so is the truth. The clock in the tower stopped at 9:40. So
everyone thought Master Ozzie died at 9:40. Everyone was *supposed* to think that.

**PEMBROOK:** *(holds up a melted candle and string)* A candle timer. The clock fell by itself. The real moment
was earlier. *(holds up the pocket watch)* The master's watch stopped when he fell. 9:21. The watch never lies.

**PEMBROOK:** And where did we find it? *(turns slowly)* Behind the desk… in the STUDY.

**[Everyone turns to look at LOLA.]**

**LOLA:** I told you, I was wrapping his midnight puzzle! He never came in!

**PEMBROOK:** *(holds up a letter)* “Lola, I know you have been secretly selling our designs to Rex's company.
At midnight, everyone will know.”

**REX:** *(gasps)* “L”… the notes were signed “L”!

**[FLASHBACK (film with a blurry or black-and-white filter): OZZIE enters the Study, holding the letter.
LOLA looks scared. She hands him the wooden puzzle box with a sweet smile. He opens it… “Ouch!”
He shakes his hand, gets dizzy, and sinks out of frame. His pocket watch drops to the floor.]**

**PEMBROOK (V.O.):** A sleeping-poison needle, hidden in the lid of his very own puzzle box. Then the secret passage
through the Library, up to the tower. Bolt the door. Light the candle timer. And back to the Ballroom, in time
for cake.

**[FLASHBACK: LOLA snips a cufflink off REX's coat on the coat rack. She tosses it into the "tower."]**

**PEMBROOK:** And then she tried to blame Mr. Hartley. But Mr. Hartley spent the whole time recording his
birthday speech.

**REX:** *(holds up phone, proudly)* Thirty-five minutes of pure genius.

**LOLA:** *(tearful)* He was going to tell everyone. I'd have lost everything!

**PEMBROOK:** You did, Miss Quill. *(He points to the door. Flashing lights, or someone with a flashlight
waving red and blue.)* The snowplow brought the police.

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** And Grandpa Ozzie's final puzzle was solved… by his grandchildren.

**[END]**

## Scene 3: The Truth, Ending 2 (VICTORIA)

> *Victoria, in the Library, with the Poisoned Cocoa, for the inheritance.*

**[The Ballroom. All six suspects in a line. PEMBROOK in front.]**

**PEMBROOK:** Midnight. The clock in the tower stopped at 9:40. So everyone thought the master died at 9:40.
Everyone was *supposed* to think that.

**PEMBROOK:** *(holds up a melted candle and string)* A candle timer. *(holds up the pocket watch)* And the master's
watch, cracked when he fell. 9:21. The watch never lies. We found it under an armchair… in the LIBRARY.

**[Everyone turns to VICTORIA.]**

**VICTORIA:** I was reading! Father never came in!

**PEMBROOK:** *(holds up a teacup)* Then why was there a Library teacup beside your chair, with cocoa in it?
Chef Bianca made only one cocoa tonight, in the blue mug, and the master brought it back empty at 9:05.

**BIANCA:** *(arms crossed)* Delicious as always, he said.

**PEMBROOK:** *(holds up the rolled-up will)* The new will. Marrowby Games, the company you've run for twenty
years, would go to Miss Quill.

**LOLA:** *(shocked)* To ME?

**[FLASHBACK (blurry or black-and-white): VICTORIA reads the will, furious. She takes a bottle from the
greenhouse shelf. In the Library she hands OZZIE a teacup with a sweet smile. “Fresh cocoa, Father.”
He sips, makes a face, gets drowsy, and slumps. His watch drops under the armchair.]**

**PEMBROOK (V.O.):** Weed killer from Miss Ginny's greenhouse. Then the bookshelf passage, up to the tower. Bolt the
door. Light the candle timer. And back to the Ballroom, in time for cake.

**[FLASHBACK: VICTORIA drops the empty bottle in the kitchen trash, looking around nervously.]**

**PEMBROOK:** And then you tried to blame Chef Bianca. But the bottle had soot on it, from the Library fireplace.

**VICTORIA:** *(slowly sits down)* Twenty years. I gave that company twenty years. And he gave it away.

**PEMBROOK:** *(gently)* And now you've given away much more, Miss Victoria. *(Flashing lights.)* The police
are here.

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** And Grandpa Ozzie's final puzzle was solved… by his grandchildren.

**[END]**

## Scene 4: The Truth, Ending 3 (AUNT GINNY)

> *Aunt Ginny, in the Greenhouse, with the Bronze Knight Trophy, for revenge for a stolen idea.*

**[The Ballroom. All six suspects in a line. PEMBROOK in front.]**

**PEMBROOK:** Midnight. The clock in the tower stopped at 9:40. So everyone thought the master died at 9:40.
Everyone was *supposed* to think that.

**PEMBROOK:** *(holds up a melted candle and string)* A candle timer. *(holds up the pocket watch)* And the master's
watch, cracked when he fell. 9:21. The watch never lies. We found it under the potting bench… in the GREENHOUSE.

**[Everyone slowly turns to AUNT GINNY.]**

**GINNY:** *(sweetly)* Me? I was watering my orchids, dear. Oswald never came in.

**PEMBROOK:** *(holds up the trophy, with a bit of dirt on it)* Then why was the Bronze Knight from the Game Room
buried in one of your flowerpots?

**FELIX:** Hey! That's from my… I mean, Dad's trophy shelf!

**PEMBROOK:** *(opens a small diary)* “Oswald stole my maze game when we were twelve and became famous. He never
once said sorry. Tonight, he will pay for it.”

**[FLASHBACK (blurry or black-and-white): Two kids at a table (or just hands) building a paper maze.
Then: GINNY in the greenhouse, holding the trophy behind her back. OZZIE walks in. She shows him an old
journal. He laughs and waves it away. He turns his back… she raises the trophy… CUT AWAY to a pot falling over.
The pocket watch rolls under the bench.]**

**PEMBROOK (V.O.):** Then the wheelbarrow, through the Library passage, up to the tower. Bolt the door. Light the
candle timer. And back to the Ballroom, in time for cake.

**[FLASHBACK: GINNY drops one of FELIX's playing cards near the clock.]**

**PEMBROOK:** And then you tried to blame your own nephew. But Master Felix was on a video call the whole time…

**FELIX:** *(sheepish)* …selling Dad's chess set. Not my finest hour.

**GINNY:** *(quietly, looking at her hands)* Sixty years, I waited for one little word. “Sorry.” And he
*laughed*.

**PEMBROOK:** *(gently)* I'm sorry, Miss Ginny. Truly. *(Flashing lights.)* But the police are here.

**NARRATOR (V.O.):** And Grandpa Ozzie's final puzzle was solved… by his grandchildren.

**[END]**
